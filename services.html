OUR SERVICES

Coordinating Healthcare, Connecting People

Biolink Solutions is licensed by the Health Professions Council of Zambia as a Digital Health Coordination and Referral Support Facility. We coordinate healthcare services through secure digital systems and strategic partnerships with licensed healthcare providers.

Our platform enables patients, healthcare facilities, laboratories, pharmacies and specialists to work together through one connected healthcare ecosystem.
